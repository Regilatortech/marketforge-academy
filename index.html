Application information

MarketForge is an educational simulation. Prices, companies, order books, performance figures and certificates shown here are simulated. This product is not a brokerage, does not execute real orders and does not provide personalized financial advice.

How MarketForge works

1. Study

Complete the Academy modules and mark lessons complete as you understand them.

2. Practice

Use virtual cash in the Simulator. Prices move automatically and the order book is simulated.

3. Review

Keep a journal entry for your decisions. The goal is to improve your process, not chase a simulated score.

4. Assess

Start a randomized exam. Questions and answer choices are shuffled each attempt.

5. Certificate

Complete all 12 modules and achieve at least 800 XP in an exam to unlock the certificate preview.
